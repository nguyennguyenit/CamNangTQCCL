# Research: Tran Duc - Kiem Bat No Truong

Date: 2026-07-09

## Summary

Anh tieng Viet ghi `Kiem Bat No Truong`. Doi chieu tieng Trung: dung la `剑拔弩张`, chien phap chi huy cua `陈翊` (Tran Duc/Tran Duc trong local). Hinh anh la ban sau buff: 70% xac suat + 70% sat thuong, khong phai ban test/cu 60%.

## Evidence

Nguon 17173 ngay 2025-09-12 ghi ban cu/test:

- `陈翊`
- `剑拔弩张`
- `指挥战法，发动率100%`
- Dong doi bi sat thuong: 60% xac suat gay sat thuong don muc tieu, moi vo tuong moi hiep toi da 3 lan.
- Khi ban than hanh dong, neu don vi phe ta co Thong soai cao nhat dang bi khong che, gay sat thuong toan dich 60%.

Nguon official Ejoy ngay 2025-11-05 ghi cap nhat can bang:

- `陈翊-剑拔弩张`
- `触发概率与伤害率由60%调整至70%`
- Neu dang bi khong che, sat thuong toan dich tu 60% tang len 108%.
- Loai sat thuong theo chi so cao hon giua Vo luc va Tri luc.

Anh user dang dung dung ban sau buff:

- Ten: `Kiem Bat No Truong`

- Loai hinh: Chi huy

- Text dau: dong doi trung sat thuong, 70% xac suat, gay 70% sat thuong, moi vo tuong moi hiep 3 lan.
