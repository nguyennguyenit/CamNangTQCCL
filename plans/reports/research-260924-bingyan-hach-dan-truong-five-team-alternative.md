## Why one replacement changes three slots

Hạch Đạn Trương uses the five-red Trương Phi from refined Đào Viên and one-red Chu Thái from ordinary Ngô Kỵ. Those two previous teams cannot coexist unchanged with it. The proposed complete portfolio keeps the three previously independent refined teams Lữ Quách Kiều, Cam Ninh Cẩm Phàm and Nữ Vương, adds refined Hạch Đạn Trương, then fills the ordinary fifth slot with exact historical Kỳ Lân Cung. It benches four-red Quan Vũ, Lưu Bị, Tôn Thượng Hương and Lăng Thống; this is a substantial red-allocation cost, not a free swap of Nữ Vương alone.

### Hạch Đạn Trương mechanics and conditions

- The [troop-mod catalog](https://sgsdeck.com/troop-mods) proposes four Hổ Vệ refinement effects corresponding to `眾志 / 善戰 / 窮追 / 武魁` (lookup labels Đồng Tâm / Thiện Chiến / Truy Kích / Võ Khôi). This is a **generic preset**, not proof that the proposed three-general/six-tactic variant wins.
- The project's trio source and the older [SGSDeck PK-S26 trio](https://sgsdeck.com/recommended/yxms-048-張飛盾潛龍) require **shield S on SP Viên Thiệu**. The account's shield-S gear and aid armor are not shown. If absent, do not present the lineup as ready at full source strength.
- Compared with the project base build, replacing Hãm Trận loses its emergency healing. The source tier/113 of the differently equipped PK-S26 team cannot be transferred to Tinh·Hổ Vệ.
- A different exact [OG Binh Diễn Hạch Đạn Cung](https://sgsdeck.com/recommended/og-bingyan-zhangfei-nuke-bow) uses **Chu Thái–Trương Phi–SP Chu Tuấn** and **Tinh·Vô Đương**, not SP Viên Thiệu/Hổ Vệ. Ownership of SP Chu Tuấn is unconfirmed, and its Thái Bình tactic would collide with the proposed ordinary Kỳ Lân if copied unchanged. Do not mix or transfer its T0/113 label to this proposed shield team.
