## Recommended Builds

### Phap Quan Khien - Luu Bi

- SP Quan Vu: Lay It Danh Nhieu + Kich Ky Noa Quy; binh thu Phan Nhi Tat Chien + Bach Chien + Khep Ho.
- SP Phap Chinh: Suc The Doi Phat + Phi Cong Che Thang/Khong Danh Ma Thang.
- Luu Bi: Ngu Lan Tran + Linh Giap May.
- Dieu kien: SP Quan Vu can S Khien va do Vo Thanh neu co.

### Phap Quan Khien - Ma Dai

- SP Quan Vu: Lay It Danh Nhieu + Kich Ky Noa Quy.
- SP Phap Chinh: Suc The Doi Phat + Phi Cong Che Thang/Khong Danh Ma Thang.
- Ma Dai: Ngu Lan Tran + Linh Giap May.
- Dung khi muon giu Luu Bi cho doi khac.

### Quan Quan Thuong

- SP Quan Vu: Trung Dung Nghia Liet + Xe Dao Chuoc Dich.
- SP Tuan Uc: Phi Cong Che Thang + Uy Muu Vo Dich.
- SP Lu Mong: Thuyen Co Muon Ten + Tam The Tran.
- Dieu kien: do do cao, do dac/specialty tot; manh giao tranh nhung dat.

### Hoang Quan Thuong

- SP Quan Vu: Trung Dung Nghia Liet + Xe Dao Chuoc Dich.
- SP Hoang Phu Tung: Thuyen Co Muon Ten + Uy Muu Vo Dich.
- SP Tuan Uc: Phi Cong Che Thang + Tam The Tran.
- Bien the tuong duong Quan Quan, thien ve on dinh/phong ngu.

## Recommendations

1. Co SP Phap Chinh: build Phap Quan Khien truoc. Chon Luu Bi neu khong bi tranh, chon Ma Dai/Hoang Phu Hoanh khi can cong ton.
2. Khong co SP Phap Chinh: uu tien Quan Quan Thuong hoac Hoang Quan Thuong neu co SP Tuan Uc + SP Lu Mong/SP Hoang Phu Tung.
3. Trang bi uu tien cho SP Quan Vu: Vo Thanh, S Khien/S Thuong theo doi, full Vo Luc; khong nen ep team neu khong co chien phap S-tier.
4. Can cap nhat app neu muon dong bo meta PK-S26: hien data local co Phap Quan Hoang Phu, Phap Quan Tieu Chi, Vũ Tung Uc/Mong, nhung chua day du ten PK-S26 nhu Phap Quan Luu Bi/Ma Dai theo Zeabur.
