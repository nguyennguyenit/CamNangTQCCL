# Binh Diễn Xuân Thu: kiểm tám nhãn đội bổ sung

- Research date: 2026-09-24 (Asia/Saigon)
- Branch: team build and name/mechanic verification
- Topic lock: Eight user-supplied team labels (`碰瓷`, `法官`, `國免`, `香香`, `藤甲`, `單揚`, `洞車槍`, `網紅大喬`) against the existing four-refined-plus-one-ordinary account plan.
- Scope: season Binh Diễn Xuân Thu per user; user server/patch unknown. User says no SP Quan Vũ or Mã Đại, and zero-red Hoàng Mã performs poorly. The six roster images show ordinary Đại Kiều and Tôn Thượng Hương, but do not establish CO Đại Kiều ownership or a complete tactic inventory.
- Subsequent evidence: [audio reassessment of the same livestream](research-260924-bingyan-video-audio-reassessment.md) confirms the speaker discusses 8,000 troops for one Chạm Sứ proposal, calls SP Quan Vũ's Đan Dương host Pháp Quan **Thuẫn**, and appears to say `先免責` (disclaimer) where the supplied label was `國免隊`. These speech observations refine the provenance below; they do not create a new account team.

## Previous conclusion and corrections

The [five-team coexistence plan](research-260923-bingyan-replace-zero-red-huangma-shop.md) remains the default. An unqualified `香香隊` or `碰瓷隊` is an archetype, not one unique three-general build.

## Decision

No sixth team or refinement reallocation is justified by these eight labels. Keep four refined teams: Đào Viên Tinh·Hãm Trận, Lữ Quách Kiều Tinh·Bạch Mã, Khương Duy Tinh·Phi Hùng, Cam Ninh Tinh·Cẩm Phàm; fifth ordinary: Chu Thái–Tôn Thượng Hương–Lăng Thống. This is an account allocation, not a current-season ranking. If using the fifth as Chạm Sứ, retain its exact six-tactic configuration and treat troop count as a matchup/terrain choice, not a verified universal 8,000 rule. Complete tactic ownership and live combat outcome remain **Chưa xác minh**.
