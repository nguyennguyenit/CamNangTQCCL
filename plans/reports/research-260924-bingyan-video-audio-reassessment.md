## Access and method

The video has no YouTube caption/automatic-caption track. At the user's explicit request the 360p video was downloaded to the system temporary directory, and FFmpeg stream-copied its audio to M4A. Local FFmpeg 8.0.1's whisper.cpp filter then generated a Chinese-language, time-coded ASR draft from the entire audio using the multilingual `ggml-large-v3-turbo-q5_0` model (official model SHA-1 verified), a 30-second context queue, and `language=zh`. The 1:34:51 MP4 and M4A each passed FFprobe duration checks. The draft is **machine recognition**, not creator captions or a corrected verbatim transcript. It often confuses Cantonese game names and similar sounds. The full ASR text is kept in the temporary workspace for checking, while this report paraphrases relevant parts instead of republishing the whole copyrighted livestream.
