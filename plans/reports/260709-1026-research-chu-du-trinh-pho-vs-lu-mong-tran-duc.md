# Research: Chu Du - Trinh Pho vs Lu Mong with Tran Duc

Date: 2026-07-09

## Summary

Neu hoi build toi uu quanh `Chu Du + Tran Duc`, nen uu tien `Lu Mong` hon `Trinh Pho` neu co du chien phap va do do. `Trinh Pho` van kha thi, nhung nghieng bien the binh dan/khac SP Ma Sieu/phan khong che, khong phai tran cao nhat.

## Findings

- Zeabur recommended list co `陈翊神火弓`: `周瑜 + 吕蒙 + 陈翊` o nhom T0, trong khi khong thay exact `周瑜 + 程普 + 陈翊` o top list.
- 925G co bien the `陈翊杀马弓`: `周瑜 + 程普 + 陈翊`, build Chu Du `夺魂挟魄 + 风助火势`, Trinh Pho `先登死士 + 盛气`, Tran Duc `暂避其锋 + 婴城`. Tac gia mo ta dung Chu Du chu dong gay sat thuong, den luot dich thi dung Tran Duc phan kich.
- 17173 co build `周瑜 + 陈翊 + 程普`, nhan manh Trinh Pho hard control va Tran Duc damage kich Chu Du, nhung build chuan cua bai la Trinh Pho `草船借箭 + 雁形阵`, Tran Duc `众志成城 + 深谋远虑`.
- 17173 bai khac sau buff goi Tran Duc la binh dan than card, phu hop nhieu team, nhung khong khang dinh Trinh Pho la toi uu hon Lu Mong.

## Recommendation

Chon `Lu Mong` neu:

- Can tran cao/meta hon.
- Co `Dang Phong Thoi Quyet` hoac chien phap khong che/giam loi ich tot.
- Muon giu doi Cung on dinh, Chu Du la damage chinh.

Chon `Trinh Pho` neu:

- Lu Mong thieu do/bi dung team khac.
- Can khac SP Ma Sieu/doi danh thuong.
- Co `Vien Tro Giap`, can hard control/keo don.
- Chap nhan team thap tran hon va phu thuoc bi danh/khong che.
