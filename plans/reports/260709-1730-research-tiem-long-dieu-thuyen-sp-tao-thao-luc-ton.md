## Findings

- 17173 ghi ro `SP貂蝉+曹操+陆逊`, khong phai `SP曹操`. Dong thoi bai khac 17173 2026-02-03 nhan manh ban `SP貂蝉+SP周瑜+老曹操`, "不是SP曹操".
- 17173 2026-04-09 danh gia ban Luc Ton co sat thuong tran cao, de ra "thien ha vo song", nhung diem yeu la so `Dang Phong Thoi Quyet` / `Thoi Phong Doan Nhan`.
- 17173 2025-11-10 co bien the `Chu Thieu + SP Dieu Thuyen + Luc Ton`: `Chu Thieu: Tiem Long + Cuong Nhu`, `SP Dieu Thuyen: Tham Tang + Biet Minh Biet Ta`, `Luc Ton: Cong Bat + Si Biet`; logic la `Cong Bat + Biet Minh + Tiem Long` bu phan giam sat thuong tu binh thu `Danh Bat Ngo Thang`.
- Local data chua co exact `SP Dieu Thuyen + SP Tao Thao + Luc Ton`.

## Recommendation

Neu co `Tao Thao thuong`, dung build 17173 truoc. Neu bat buoc dung `SP Tao Thao`, xem la bien the thuc nghiem: co the de `SP Tao Thao` cam `Tiem Long Tran + Thuyen Co Muon Ten` hoac `An Ui Quan Dan/Cat Xuong`, nhung do tin cay thap hon ban old Cao Cao vi chua co source build chuan.
