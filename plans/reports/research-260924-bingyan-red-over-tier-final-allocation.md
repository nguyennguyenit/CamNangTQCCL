## Decision and evidence

The previous third team, Khương Duy–Tào Tháo–Mãn Sủng Tinh·Phi Hùng, used four visible red marks, all on Mãn Sủng. The alternative Nữ Vương Thuẫn has nine visible marks from five-red Trương Xuân Hoa and four-red Mãn Sủng, with Tư Mã Ý the uninvested damage core. The user's explicit red-over-tier priority makes the higher-red defensive shell preferable as the fourth investment **if** Trương Xuân Hoa can reach shield S and the tactics are owned. This is an account-priority inference, not evidence that the adapted team wins more games in Global Binh Diễn.

The exact [SGSDeck PK-S24 Nữ Vương Thuẫn](https://sgsdeck.com/recommended/team-dnyx2) has Tư Mã Ý: Sĩ Biệt Tam Nhật + Toàn Quân Đồng Lòng; Trương Xuân Hoa: Phong Thi Trận + Mê Hoặc; Mãn Sủng: Dưỡng Sức Đợi Chiến + Đằng Giáp Binh. It calls for a shield treasure on Trương Xuân Hoa and aid armor. The video creator rejects Nữ Vương with Đan Dương around [52:49–53:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0c_7C-7mi-0&t=3169s) and favors going back to Đằng Giáp in that comparison. ## Why other directions are not the default

- **Previous Khương Duy Phi Hùng:** exact [OG source](https://sgsdeck.com/recommended/og-bingyan-jiangwei-feixiong-cavalry), but a white damage core and only four visible red marks. Keep as a source-complete fallback if shield-S equipment or the Nữ Vương tactics are absent, or local fire matchups make Đằng Giáp a poor choice. It shares Mãn Sủng and two tactics with Nữ Vương, so these are alternatives, not simultaneous teams.
- **Normal Lữ Mông 5 red:** a [community OG-season Cam Ninh–Thái Sử Từ–normal Lữ Mông bow](https://sgsdeck.com/teams/kOqg-4xn) exists, but its listed T0/120 comes from one user rating, its six tactics differ substantially from the exact OG SP Lữ Mông team, and its Phong Thi conflicts with Nữ Vương. A one-for-one substitution would change the configuration and has no verified score. Keep normal Lữ Mông available for a separate tested variant, not as a silent replacement for SP.
- **Chu Thái–Trương Phi–SP Viên Thiệu Hổ Vệ:** uses eight visible red marks but consumes Trương Phi from invested Đào Viên and Chu Thái from ordinary Ngô Kỵ. Its old source tier belongs to another season and exact tactic set; see [comparison](research-260923-chu-thai-truong-phi-sp-vien-thieu-alternative.md).
- **Hoàng Mã and SP Quan Vũ Pháp Quan:** the former's zero-red core already underperforms for this user; the latter requires two explicitly absent generals. Video mentions and historical T0 labels do not override those account facts.
