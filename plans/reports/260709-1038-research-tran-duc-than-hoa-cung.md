# Research: Tran Duc Than Hoa Cung

Date: 2026-07-09

## Summary

`Tran Duc Than Hoa Cung` tuong ung Chinese `陈翊神火弓`. Build chuan hien tai la `Chu Du + Lu Mong + Tran Duc` (`周瑜 + 吕蒙 + 陈翊`), phe Ngo, Cung. Zeabur xep T0, 113 diem, PK-S26 Anh Hung Menh The.

Local `data/teams.json` chua co exact team nay. Local dang co cac nhom Tran Duc khac: `Chu Du + Lo Tuc + Tran Duc`, `Chu Du + Luc Ton + Tran Duc`, `Gia Cat Luong + Bang Thong + Tran Duc`, `Truong Phi + Quan Vu + Tran Duc`.

## Mechanism

- Chu Du la damage chinh. `Doat Hon` hut chi so, `Thuong Binh`/`Phong Tro` tao sat thuong phap.
- Lu Mong tao dam thuong va pha phong thu bang `Duong Phong`, dong thoi `Toan Quan` tang hoi/phu tro.
- Tran Duc dung `Kiem Bat No Truong` de phan kich khi dong doi bi danh; neu tuong Thong cao nhat bi khong che, den luot Tran Duc se kich AOE 108% sau buff.
- `Tinh·Phong Thi Tran` day damage Chu Du, lam team co dang Than Hoa ro hon.

## Recommendations

Build uu tien neu co du chien phap:

1. `Chu Du`: `Doat Hon Hiep Phach + Thuong Binh Phat Muu`
2. `Lu Mong`: `Duong Phong Thoi Quyet + Toan Quan Dong Long`
3. `Tran Duc`: `Tinh·Phong Thi Tran + Tham Muu Vien Luoc`

Neu thieu `Thuong Binh`: dung `Phong Tro Hoa The`.

Neu thieu `Tham Muu Vien Luoc`: dung `Toa Nhue`.

Khong nen thay Lu Mong bang Trinh Pho neu muc tieu la tran cao; Trinh Pho la bien the phan khong che/khac danh thuong, khong phai ban `Tran Duc Than Hoa Cung` chuan.
