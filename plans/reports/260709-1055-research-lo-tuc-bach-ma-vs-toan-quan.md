## Summary

Ly do: exact local team `do-uy-ss18-0` ghi `Lo Tuc: Tinh·Phong Thi Tran + Toan Quan Dong Long / Bach Ma Nghia Tong`, tuc `Toan Quan` la main, `Bach Ma` la alternative. ## Tactic Comparison

### Bach Ma Nghia Tong

Local effect:

- Binh chung Cung.
- Tang hanh quan.
- 2 hiep dau toan doi co Tien Cong.
- Tang ty le phat dong chien phap chu dong.

Official/Ejoy analysis:

- Hop doi chu dong burst manh trong 2 hiep dau.
- Neu 2 hiep dau khong tao chenh lech binh luc vi giam thuong/bi khong che/khong ra skill thi ve sau gan nhu thieu mot chien phap, chien ton tang.

Trong team nay, `Bach Ma` chu yeu buff `Chu Du` vi `Tran Duc` dang dung chien phap chi huy/giam ich, `Lo Tuc` la support. Loi ich co nhung khong full value nhu team co 3 tuong chu dong burst.

### Toan Quan Dong Long

Nguon Ali213:

- Trong chien dau, moi hiep co xac suat tri lieu vo tuong phe ta it linh nhat.
- Dong thoi gay mưu damage len don vi dich ngau nhien.
- Xac suat bi anh huong boi tong Tri luc phe ta; tri lieu/sat thuong bi anh huong boi Tri luc.

Trong team `Chu Du + Lo Tuc + Tran Duc`, ca doi nghieng Tri/Thong, nen `Toan Quan` co gia tri:

- Bu sustain khi khong co Thuyen Co/Cat Xuong.
- Giup doi song den khi Tran Duc tich phan damage.
- Van co damage phu, khong chi la heal.

## Recommendation

Default:

Dung `Bach Ma Nghia Tong` khi:

- Muon danh nhanh/can tien cong.
- Chu Du do cao, can snowball 2 hiep dau.
- Server it doi giam thuong/hut sat thuong/sustain dai.
- Dung team nhu doi phu lay chien cong, chap nhan chien ton.

Dung `Toan Quan Dong Long` khi:

- Chu Du/Tran Duc it do hoac can on dinh.
- Doi nay la doi chinh hoac can danh lien chien.
- Server nhieu burst, SP Ma Sieu, doi danh thuong, doi phap dai hoi.
- Da co `Thinh Khi + Toa Nhue` tren Tran Duc, can them sustain hon la them nhip no dau.
